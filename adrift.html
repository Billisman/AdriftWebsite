Adrift

After a fatal accident, a young astrophysics student awakens on a mysterious tropical archipelago caught between life and death. Believing he can escape his fate, he journeys across islands shaped by the stages of grief, collecting fragments of his past and confronting the memories they hold. Guided by a strange creature lurking at the edge of his vision, he must ultimately come to terms with his unfinished life, his lost future, and the reality of his own death..
