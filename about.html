Who We Are

Adrift is a story-driven adventure game set on a mysterious tropical archipelago between life and death. Players follow a young astrophysics student as he journeys through islands inspired by the stages of grief, uncovering memories of his past and confronting the reality of his fate.Created by a team of 30 students as part of our minor program, the project brings together art, design, programming, writing, and production in a shared effort to tell a meaningful story.
